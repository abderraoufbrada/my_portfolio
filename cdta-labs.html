Research Laboratories & Advanced Technologies

A closer look at the scientific environment, research activities and technological platforms of the Centre for the Development of Advanced Technologies.

Centre for the Development of Advanced Technologies
01 / The experience

Beyond the classroom.

Visiting CDTA offered an opportunity to discover the research environment behind advanced technologies and to see how scientific concepts are developed through experimental work and technological platforms.

The centre brings together different research fields, including microelectronics, nanotechnology, optics, photonics, laser technologies and robotics.

ABOUT CDTA The Centre for the Development of Advanced Technologies is a public scientific and technological institution dedicated to scientific research, technological innovation, development and training.
Research area / 01

Microelectronics & Nanotechnology

Semiconductor devices / Integrated circuits / MEMS
01

Engineering at the microscopic scale

This research area focuses on the design, fabrication and characterization of electronic devices and integrated circuits.

Its activities include semiconductor device characterization, analog and RF integrated circuits, VLSI design, MEMS and sensor microsystems.

Semiconductors MEMS RF Circuits Sensors VLSI
Research area / 02

Optics & Photonics

Light / Optical systems / Photonic devices
02

Exploring the science of light

Research in optics and photonics involves the study and development of optical systems, light-based technologies and photonic devices.

These technologies have applications in sensing, measurement, imaging and advanced instrumentation.

Optical Systems Photonics Light Optical Sensing
Research area / 03

Ionized Media & Lasers

Plasma physics / Laser technologies / Materials
03

Interaction between light and matter

This research field investigates laser systems, plasma diagnostics and the interaction of laser radiation with materials.

Research activities also include material synthesis, physicochemical characterization and laser-based material processing.

Laser Systems Plasma Materials Science LIBS
Research area / 04

Robotics & Industrial Automation

Control / Embedded Systems / Automation
04

From intelligent systems to real applications

Research activities cover robotics, autonomous navigation, dynamic system control and automated production systems.

These fields combine mechanical systems, electronics, embedded computing and control engineering.

Robotics Automation Embedded Systems Control
