Industrial Visit

Field Visit to Seawater Desalination Plant

July 3, 2024  |  Fouka, Tipaza Province, Algeria  |  1 Day

Objective

To provide students with practical exposure to real-world water treatment infrastructure and understand the engineering processes involved in seawater desalination.

Activities & Insights Gained

  • Plant Tour: Comprehensive visit to the various sections of the Fouka seawater desalination station
  • Desalination Process: Detailed explanation of the multi-stage desalination process using chemical and physical protocols
  • Technical Operations: Understanding of water intake, pre-treatment, reverse osmosis, post-treatment, and brine discharge systems
  • Interactive Session: Direct engagement with the General Manager who provided in-depth explanations and answered all questions

Technologies & Processes

Seawater Intake
Pre-Treatment
Reverse Osmosis
Chemical Treatment

Key Takeaways

This field visit offered valuable practical insight into one of Algeria’s important desalination facilities. Students gained a clear understanding of how theoretical knowledge in chemistry, physics, and engineering is applied in large-scale water desalination operations to address national water security challenges.

Acknowledgments
