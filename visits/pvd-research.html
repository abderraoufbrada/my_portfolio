Discovery & Practice

Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) Laboratory

March 2026  |  Research Center Name  |  2 Weeks

Objective

Discover and practice thin-film deposition techniques used in semiconductor manufacturing, optical coatings, and surface engineering applications.

Activities & Techniques Learned

  • Evaporation PVD: Thermal evaporation of metals (Al, Au, Cr) in high vacuum
  • Sputtering: DC and RF magnetron sputtering for compound materials
  • System Operation: Vacuum pump operation, base pressure achievement, gas flow control
  • Film Characterization: Thickness measurement, adhesion testing, optical inspection
  • Safety Protocols: Handling high-voltage systems, cryogenic liquids, toxic materials

Equipment Used

Electron Beam Evaporator
Magnetron Sputtering System
Quartz Crystal Monitor
Optical Microscope

Key Takeaways

This hands-on experience deepened my understanding of thin-film physics and the critical parameters controlling deposition quality. I gained practical skills in vacuum technology and appreciation for precision manufacturing in research environments.
