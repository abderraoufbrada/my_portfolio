Academia & Industry

Field Visit to Condor Academy & Manufacturing Plant

2025-2026 Academic Year  |  Condor Academy, Bordj Bou Arréridj, Algeria  |  1 Day

Objective

Strengthen the link between academic knowledge and professional experience by connecting theoretical nanoscience concepts with real-world industrial manufacturing processes at one of Algeria's leading technology groups.

Activities & Insights Gained

  • Condor Academy Tour: Exploration of training facilities dedicated to professional development and technical education within the Condor Group ecosystem
  • Manufacturing Plant Visit: Direct observation of production lines for air conditioners, refrigerators, and televisions at a Condor Group industrial branch
  • Professional Engagement: Interactive sessions with industry professionals discussing operational workflows and quality management systems
  • Process Management: Deep insights into manufacturing environment operations, supply chain logistics, and industrial management strategies
  • Skills Development: Collaborative learning exercises and adaptability training in a professional industrial setting

Sectors Explored

Air Conditioning Systems
Refrigeration Technology
Consumer Electronics (TV)
Industrial Automation

Key Takeaways

This enriching experience successfully bridged the gap between classroom theory and industrial practice. We developed a practical understanding of manufacturing workflows, enhanced our collaborative learning capabilities, and gained valuable adaptability skills essential for the modern engineering landscape. The visit reinforced how nanotechnology and advanced materials science underpin innovations in consumer appliance manufacturing and Industry 4.0 transformation.

Acknowledgments

We extend our sincere thanks to Condor Academy for their warm welcome and insightful presentations, and to the National School of Nanoscience & Nanotechnology (Sidi Abdellah, Algiers, Algeria) administration for organizing this impactful visit that strengthens our academic-industry partnership.
