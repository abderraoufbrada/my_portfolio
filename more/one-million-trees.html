One Million Trees in One Day

October 25, 2025  |  Mektaa Khira Forest, Sidi Abdellah, Algiers  |  1 Day

Objective

Participate in Algeria’s ambitious national campaign to plant **one million trees in a single day**, contributing to environmental protection, climate resilience, and sustainable development across the country.

Activities & Insights Gained

  • National Campaign: Part of the nationwide initiative organized by the Ministry of Agriculture, Rural Development & Fisheries and the General Directorate of Forests.
  • Planting Session: Actively participated in planting tree seedlings at Mektaa Khira Forest (مقطع خيرة), Sidi Abdellah area.
  • Collective Effort: Joined thousands of volunteers, public officials, civil society organizations, and environmental advocates.
  • Local Impact: Approximately 12,000 trees were planted at this specific site in one day.
  • Environmental Awareness: Learned about the importance of reforestation for combating desertification and enhancing biodiversity.

Partners & Organizers

Ministry of Agriculture Rural Development & Fisheries
General Directorate of Forests
Location Mektaa Khira Forest, Sidi Abdellah
Volunteers +12,000 trees planted locally

Key Takeaways

This campaign was a powerful demonstration of collective environmental action. Participating in such a large-scale national initiative reinforced the importance of community involvement in fighting climate change and preserving Algeria’s natural heritage.

It was inspiring to see strong collaboration between government institutions, civil society, and young volunteers working toward a greener and more sustainable future.

Acknowledgments

We extend our gratitude to the Ministry of Agriculture, Rural Development & Fisheries and the General Directorate of Forests for launching this impactful national campaign.

Special thanks to all volunteers, organizers, and environmental advocates who made this day a success.
